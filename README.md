# Stalking Log

A deer log for your phone: **https://ondacreative.github.io/stalking-log/**

- Log each deer: species, sex, age, larder weight, time, place, shot details, notes, photo.
- "Use my location" fills in the place from the phone's GPS.
- Wind, weather, light and moon at each deer are worked out for you.
- The top card shows the wind and light where you are now, with shooting hours.

## Saving from your phone

Mark sends a set-up link. Tap it once and this phone can save.
New phone, or cleared the browser? Ask Mark for the link again.

## Worth knowing

- The log is public. Anyone with the link can see it, including the map pins.
- Shooting hours shown are the England and Wales rule: 1 hour before sunrise to 1 hour after sunset.
  Check the rules where you stalk.
- Records are in `data/culls.json`. Photos are in `photos/`.
