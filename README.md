# Stalking Log

A deer log for your phone. It saves to this GitHub repository.

- Log each deer: species, sex, age, larder weight, time, place, shot details, notes, photo.
- "Use my location" fills in the place from the phone's GPS.
- Wind, weather, light and moon at each deer are worked out for you.
- The top card shows the wind and light where you are now, with shooting hours.

## Setting it up (10 minutes, once)

1. Make a free account at github.com, if you don't have one.
2. Tap **New repository**. Name it `stalking-log`. Set it to **Public**. Create it.
3. Tap **uploading an existing file**. Drag in everything from this folder:
   `index.html`, `store.js`, `conditions.js`, `README.md` and the `data` folder.
   Tap **Commit changes**.
4. Open **Settings**, then **Pages**. Under "Branch", pick **main** and **/ (root)**. Save.
5. Wait 2 minutes. Your log is at `https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/stalking-log/`.
   Add it to your phone's home screen.
6. To save from your phone, open the page and follow **Saving from this phone** at the bottom.

## Worth knowing

- The repository is public. Anyone with the link can see the log, including the map pins.
- Shooting hours shown are the England and Wales rule: 1 hour before sunrise to 1 hour after sunset.
  Check the rules where you stalk.
- Records are in `data/culls.json`. Photos are in `photos/`.
